# ARINC717Forge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

ARINC 717 is the mandatory data bus standard mandated by global aviation civil authorities (FAA, EASA, ICAO) for all transport aircraft crash-survivable flight data recording. Traditional FDR ground readout and telemetry analysis equipment (Teledyne Controls, L3Harris, Avionics Interface Technologies) requires \$15,000 to \$40,000 per hardware license. ARINC717Forge Pro delivers an accessible, browser-native simulation, analysis, and protocol compliance audit environment for airline flight data monitoring (FDM) teams, accident investigation boards, and avionics integration engineers.

## 2\. Commercial Pricing Tiers

- **Flight Data Analysis (FDM) Engineer Seat**: \$149/month (Interactive studio, headless Python CLI, parameter de-commutator).  
- **Airline & MRO Fleet Safety Lab License**: \$1,490/month (\$12,500/year perpetual) including custom flight parameter frame mapping, FAA Part 121 compliance reports, and priority engineering support.
